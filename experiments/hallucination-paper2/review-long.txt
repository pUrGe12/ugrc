    ## Methods summary: BA@HGNRs probe and the single-probe CD/RGB sensor array
    
    **Upstream conditions for HGNR growth (from the optimisation in Results).** The full HGNR synthesis is only described in the Supporting Information. The main text does give the chosen growth conditions: a cysteine concentration of 75 μM[1] and an Au/Ag growth solution volume of 80 μL[2].
    
    **Probe preparation (BA@HGNRs), in order:**
    1. Centrifuge the CTAB-stabilised HGNRs (6800 rpm, 10 min[3]) and redisperse them in an equal volume of KOH solution (pH 11[4]).
    2. Mix 320 μL[5] of this solution with 6.4 μL[6] of mercaptophenylboronic acid (BA) solution (10 mM in ethanol solvent[7]) in a 1.5 mL tube[8]. The optimised BA concentration is 165 μM[9].
    3. Incubate at 25 ◦C for 12 h[10] under slow stirring (300 rpm[11]) in the dark.
    4. Centrifuge at 3600 rpm for 10 min[12], then resuspend and concentrate in 160 μL of 10 mM NH4HCO3[13].
    5. Store the probes at 4 ◦C[14] until bacterial detection.
    
    **Sensing protocol, in order:**
    1. Culture each bacterial strain at 37 ◦C for 24 h[15]. Collect the cells by centrifugation (no speed or time is given), then wash with sterile 0.9% NaCl[16] solution followed by ultrapure sterile water.
    2. Adjust the optical density at 600 nm to 0.1[17]. Plate counts at this optical density gave: 9.2 × 10^7 CFU/mL[18] (P. aeruginosa), 8.5 × 10^7 CFU/mL[19] (E. coli), 7.6 × 10^7 CFU/mL[20] (E. sakazakii), 6.4 ×10^7 CFU/mL[21] (S. typhimurium), 7.4 ×10^7 CFU/mL[22] (E. faecium), 7.1 ×10^7 CFU/mL[23] (L. monocytogenes) and 8.2 ×10^7 CFU/[24]mL (S. aureus).
    3. Mix 160 μL[25] of bacterial suspension with an equal volume of BA@HGNRs solution in a 1.5 mL tube at 37 ◦C for 15 min[26].
    4. Add 30 μL[27] of NaCl solution to reach a final concentration of 65 mM[28]. This was the optimum found when NaCl was varied from 25 mM to 65 mM[29] and on to 95 mM[30].
    5. Read the colour (RGB) with a smartphone colour-picker app, then record the CD spectrum and run LDA on the data. For the concentration-variation tests, bacteria were used at OD600 =0.02–0.2[31].
    
    The live/dead and serum (spiked and clinical) procedures are only described in the Supporting Information, so the main text gives no numeric parameters for them.
