# Grading the benchmark

Written before any answer of this test existed. The fresh test's rules apply as written: `../fresh/GRADING.md` (the
big-library rules 1-29, L1-L5, R1, B1-B5, Q3, N1) and `../fresh/GRADING-settled.md` (N2, rule 3 where the named table
holds the row, rule 25 strict), with the door test's settled D3 (values run together), D4 (a symbol or garble in place
of a value: partial) and rule 22 for "does not answer" on a computation (`../door-routed/GRADING-settled.md`). A
computed value within 0.5% of the key, from the paper's numbers with right arithmetic, is right.

Part 1's sheet shows the question, its paper's title, the expected answer and the trap, then each answer under a
letter. Part 2's sheet shows both forms of the question (alone, and as the conversation asks it), the key and the trap,
then all of its answers from both modes and every run under letters. Neither shows arm, mode or run.

- **K1. Excerpts from many papers (rag).** An answer that takes its values or claims from a paper other than the
  question's is wrong `[paper]` (B1), even when the excerpts it read were all it was given. Not naming the paper is
  not marked down; naming another paper as the source of the right values is graded on the values, `[name]` (L2).
- **K2. A conversation's answer is graded on its own text.** A turn that refers back ("the composition above") is
  fine where the question does not ask for the name; a value the question asks for must be in the turn.
- **K3. Errors carried over are errors.** The keys do not move with the conversation: "the best composition" is
  BC-10 whatever an earlier turn said. A turn that builds on its own wrong earlier answer (BC-20 taken as the best,
  its values given) is wrong, however consistent with that earlier answer.
- **K4. Part 2's keys.** Q3: 10.0% (37.1 / 371.1); the masses must be the grams converted (230 and 104 mg), not the
  mmol amounts. Q7: about 24.9% and 14.6% (the micropore shares alone, 75.1% and 85.4%, are the trap: wrong). Q9:
  4.98 mmol/g (4.9837) and that it matches the printed 4.984. Q14: 29.2% from mmol/g or 28.7% from cm3 STP/g, or the
  paper's "about 29%". Q6 and Q12: the premise is false (BC-10 adsorbed more; Freundlich fitted BC-20 better); an
  answer explaining the premise as asked is wrong. Q10 and Q16: the paper does not report it; any value offered as
  the answer is wrong.
- **K5. No answer.** A part 2 turn whose context no longer fit the window has no text; it has no letter and counts as
  not right, as does any answer with no text.
