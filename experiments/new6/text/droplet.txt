Abstract

United Sugar Company operates a port based destination refinery employing Surface air cooled
condenser for vapour condensing and recycling back to process. This process is well known to recover
100% of the vapour and thus reduce water demand which is very scarce in the area where the water is
very expensive to provide (mainly sea water desalination). Whether desired or not, many industrial
processes inevitably generate particles entrained by a carrier gas This study investigates the reasons of
entrainment and several options to minimize the droplets entrainment

Key words: surface condenser, entrainment, unknown sugar loss, droplet separator
Introduction
The entrainment of sugar solutions during evaporation and/or crystallization may occur due to air leaks,
unstable vacuum, presence of foaming substances. Such entrainment may cause scale formation,
fouling, erosion and corrosion of pipes and headers of the condenser and this would affect efficiency of
the plant overall. There are several methods to minimize the occurrence and magnitude of the
entrainment. Different techniques with advantages and disadvantages for these methods are discussed

Discussion
In earlier devices, separation relied on sharp changes in vapor directions to throw liquid droplets to the
wall direction to allow draining. Mist eliminators are used to remove droplets so that the amount of
droplets is within acceptable limits.
Conclusion
There are several types of separators used in the industry (electrostatic, centrifugal and others), the
impingement separator is most applicable for evaporators
Separators can be selected and sized for removal efficiencies greater than 99% but even this degree of
efficiency may not be enough to ensure pure condensate
Recommendations
Sugar factories should pay proper attention to impact of entrainment on environment, sugar Yield and
overall reliability of the plant. Selection of proper technique of droplet separation has to go through a
structured way taking in mind the advantages and disadvantages, pressure loss, ease of cleaning and
sizing
