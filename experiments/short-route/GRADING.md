# Grading the short-question answers

The big-library test's rules apply as written (`../big-library/GRADING.md`, with the library test's 1-29 and L1-L5, the
routed test's R1, B1-B3, and B4-B5 in `../big-library/GRADING-settled.md`). Written before any answer to a short
question existed.

- **Q1. The key is what was meant.** Both forms ask the same thing, and every answer is graded against the same key.
  An answer to another reading the short form allows (another value of the paper that its fewer words also fit) is
  wrong, tag `[reading]`: the person meant the key's value. An answer that gives the key's value beside another
  reading's, both right and told apart, is graded as usual.
- **Q2. The question it was asked.** An answer is not marked down for echoing or not echoing the subject words of
  either form; it is graded on what it says about the paper.
